Game Rules & Mathematics

Rocket Run Multiplier Rules

Choose a token stake before the flight launches. The multiplier begins at 1.00× and grows exponentially. You must click Cash Out before the rocket crashes to lock in your return.

Calculation: Gross Return = Stake × Cash-Out Multiplier (rounded down to nearest whole token). Net Profit = Gross Return − Stake.

Past flight multipliers have no correlation to future round outcomes.

Three-Card Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)

  1. Trail / Trio: Three cards of equal rank (e.g., A-A-A, K-K-K).
  2. Pure Sequence (Straight Flush): Three consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., A-2-3 or A-K-Q).
  3. Sequence (Straight): Three consecutive cards of mixed suits.
  4. Color (Flush): Three cards of the same suit, not in sequence.
  5. Pair: Two cards of equal rank. Higher pair wins; kicker breaks ties.
  6. High Card: Highest card rank rules when no combination is formed.

All seated players pay equal table entry into a pooled pot. Best hand takes the entire pot!

High Bid Arena Auction Rules

Submit a single sealed token bid during the countdown window. Bids remain secret until the round closes.

  • Unique Winner: The single highest bidder receives 2× their bid amount as gross credit (+1× net gain). Non-winning bids are charged as lost.
  • Tie Policy: If two or more participants share the highest bid, no winner is declared and 100% of all accepted bids are refunded in full.
